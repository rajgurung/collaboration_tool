1. "Delete message" in the message sheet

Juniper Tea Co. In #general (ana, leo, maya, tom), tom means to DM ana about a wrong price and posts it to everyone instead. He taps the bubble. The sheet is today's, with "Delete photo" replaced by "Delete message". Frames labelled "dark" stay dark; the rest follow the theme switch at the top.

Decisions

  1. One red row, "Delete message", in its own list block at the very bottom. Same place, icon and style as the "Delete photo" row it replaces.
  2. The sheet never shows both. A photo message gets "Delete message" here; "Delete photo" (keep the caption) stays only in the full-screen viewer.
  3. Shown to the sender, and to owners and admins in the conversation. Anyone else gets today's sheet with no greyed-out row.
  4. The thread page sheet has no "Reply in thread", so "Delete message" sits right under the reactions.
  5. A deleted message opens no sheet, so there is no "already deleted" sheet to design.

2. The confirm

One confirm, built like the photo one. The title and the quote say what goes; the line under them grows only with what applies: where, the photo, Documents, the replies. It always ends "This can't be undone."

Phone · text only, #general
Phone · with a photo, #finance
Phone · #general with a photo (maya, admin), dark
Phone · thread parent with 3 replies (maya)
Phone · DM, leo to ana

Decisions

  1. Title "Delete this message?" for every kind, photo or not. The extra line says the photo goes too.
  2. Quote reuses the sheet's .quote: who, when, two lines of the text. "Photo · caption" when there is one. The admin sees whose it is.
  3. Sentences stack in a fixed order: where, photo, Documents, replies, can't be undone. One paragraph, so it reads as one thought.
  4. The Documents line only appears with a photo in #general. A text-only #general message has no file.
  5. Thread parent (owners and admins only): "Its 3 replies are deleted too." ("Its reply is deleted too." for one). A reply's own confirm has no such line.
  6. Solid red "Delete" and outline "Cancel", full width, as photo delete.

3. "Message deleted" everywhere it showed

Superseded by hard delete: the bubble is removed instead. The bubble keeps its place, author and time, and loses everything else. Open feeds and thread pages swap it live. The chat list, Home and the bell are right on the next load.

Phone · maya's open #general, updated live, dark
Phone · thread, parent deleted, replies kept
Phone · thread, leo deleted his reply
Phone · ana's chat list
Phone · ana's Home, Recent chat
Phone · ana's notifications, dark
Phone · ana had the photo open in #finance

Decisions

  1. The bubble drops its fill (yours and theirs) and keeps a thin outline. Inside: trash icon and "Message deleted" in the "Photo removed" style, then the time. No tick, no reactions, no "Tap" target.
  2. A deleted parent keeps its "3 replies" footer, which still opens the thread. The thread page shows the same placeholder at the top, then the "3 replies" divider and the replies.
  3. A deleted reply stays in its place in the thread, so the replies after it still make sense. The reply count does not change.
  4. Chat list and Home: "tom: Message deleted" or "You: Message deleted", only when it was the last message. Italic, like the bubble.
  5. Bell: the notice keeps its headline ("tom mentioned you in #general") and link. Its quote line becomes "Message deleted". It keeps its read or unread state.
  6. Toast "Message deleted" for the person who deleted, as "Photo deleted" today. Nobody else gets one.
  7. The viewer reuses today's "Photo removed" state. The caption goes too, since the whole message is gone.

4. Busy and failed

Same behaviour as the photo confirm, with "message" in the words.

Phone · deleting
Phone · couldn't delete
Phone · already deleted elsewhere
Phone · sheet open when it was deleted, dark

Decisions

  1. Deleting: spinner and "Deleting..." on the red button; both buttons lock; the sheet can't be dismissed until the answer comes.
  2. Failure: red alert inside the confirm, the message stays, "Delete" is the retry. A toast only if the confirm was already closed.
  3. Already deleted (someone else got there first): the alert says so and the buttons become one "Close". Retrying would only fail again.
  4. A sheet left open when the delete lands: the quote turns to "Message deleted", reactions and rows go, and one line says what happened. Close is the only action. It does not say who deleted it.
