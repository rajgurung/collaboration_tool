1. Picking a photo, adding a caption, sending

#finance, a group with ana, maya and tom. Maya taps the photo button and picks her fuel receipt. Nothing sends until she taps Send. Frames labelled "Dark" stay dark; the rest follow the theme switch at the top.

Phone · send failed, dark

Decisions

  1. The preview sits inside the composer, above the text box, as a 72px rounded tile. The feed stays visible above it.
  2. Next to the tile: the file name and size. Size matters because of the 25 MB limit and phone data.
  3. The X is a 24px dark dot on the tile's corner with a 44px tap area. Tapping it clears the photo and sends nothing. Anything typed stays.
  4. The text box gets focus and reads "Add a caption". It is no longer required, so Send works with no text.
  5. The photo button stays visible. Picking again swaps the photo, since a message holds one.
  6. Sending: a spinner over the tile and "Sending..." beside it. No percentage: the upload uses fetch, which cannot report progress. The X hides, the caption locks, Send shows a spinner and does nothing. Enter does nothing either.
  7. Failed: the tile gets a coral edge and an alert mark, and the line beside it says what happened and what to do. Photo and caption stay. Send is the retry; no extra button.
  8. No toast for a failed send. A toast fades; this line stays until you retry or remove the photo.
  9. No half-sent bubble in the feed. The photo appears once, when it arrives over the socket, as today.

2. A photo message in the conversation

One message: the photo on top, the caption under it, the time in the corner. The bubble is the same as today. The only change is what tapping the photo does.

Phone · #finance
Phone · #finance, dark

Decisions

  1. Same 4:3 photo box as today (320px wide at most), so the feed does not jump as photos load.
  2. Tapping the photo opens the viewer. Tapping the caption or the bubble edge opens the message sheet, as today. The photo stays a real link, so it still works if the script fails to load.
  3. Desktop: the pointer turns into a zoom-in cursor over the photo, and the photo dims slightly on hover. That is how you can tell it is a separate target from the bubble.
  4. A photo with no caption keeps today's short line under it for the time and ticks.
  5. Screen readers hear "Photo from maya. Open full screen" in place of "Opens in a new tab".
  6. "Photo removed" stays as it is. It only happens in #general, where photos can be deleted from Documents.

3. The full-screen viewer

Ana taps maya's receipt on her phone. It opens over the chat, in the same tab. Close, Escape, a tap on the dark area or the phone's back button bring her back to #finance where she was.

Phone · receipt, with caption
Phone · wide photo, no caption
Phone · loading
Phone · couldn't load

Decisions

  1. Always a near-black backdrop (#0b0b0c, the dark canvas colour), in light and dark mode. Photos read true on black, and the viewer feels like a separate layer. This is the one screen that looks the same in both themes.
  2. One bar on top: Close (X) on the left where Back usually sits, then the sender's avatar, name, and "Today 09:40 · #finance". Download on the right.
  3. Phone: Download is an icon button to save room. Desktop: a labelled "Download" button, with Close at the far right where people look for it on a computer.
  4. The photo is fitted: whole photo visible, never cropped, never upscaled past its own size. Tall receipts get dark bars at the sides; wide photos at the top and bottom.
  5. The caption sits under the photo, full length, in readable white text. Long captions scroll inside their area (at most about a quarter of the screen) so the photo keeps most of the space.
  6. No caption, no caption area. The photo gets the room.
  7. Loading: the thumbnail is shown at once if the phone still has it; if not, a spinner in the middle. The bar is already there, so Close always works.
  8. Couldn't load: a plain message and "Try again". Download is still there in case the full file works.
  9. No swipe, no zoom, no next or previous, as the brief says.

4. The photo button in every conversation

#general, every group (old and new) and every direct message now have the photo button, in the same place: between the text box and Send. Thread replies do not get it yet.

Phone · chat list
Phone · #finance, group
Phone · DM with tom, empty
Phone · DM with tom, dark

Decisions

  1. The composer is identical everywhere: smiley inside the text box, photo button, Send. Only the placeholder changes: "Message #finance", "Message tom".
  2. The empty state of a new conversation is today's "Start the conversation". Its line now reads "Share an update, a photo, or ask a question", so people know photos work here.
  3. The chat list shows a photo message as "Photo" with a small image icon, followed by the caption if there is one: "maya: Photo · Van fuel £62".
  4. No "private" badge on group or DM photos. They already behave the way people expect: only the people in the chat see them.
  5. Thread page: unchanged, no photo button. A photo message can still start a thread from its message sheet.

The chat list "Photo" preview is a small copy change in the list template. If the producer wants to keep the PR tight, it can be dropped; today a captionless photo shows an empty preview line.

5. Errors and roles

Owner, admin and member see the same screens. What decides access is being in the conversation, not the role. An owner who is not in #finance does not see #finance, and gets "not found" at a photo's address.

Picked a photo over 25 MB (toast, no tile)
Too big: 31.4 MB. The limit is 25 MB. Try saving a smaller copy.
Picked a HEIC, GIF or other file (toast, no tile)
That file type can't be sent. Use a JPG, PNG or WebP photo.
Server refused the photo (422, line under the tile)

Decisions

  1. Problems found before sending (too big, wrong type) use today's toast and never show a tile. There is nothing to retry.
  2. Problems during or after sending show on the tile line, so the photo and caption stay put.
  3. A server message (422) replaces the default line word for word, as today.
  4. A stale tab (wrong organisation) keeps today's behaviour: the page reloads into the right place. The picked photo is lost; that is rare and acceptable.
  5. Removed from a group while the viewer is open: the next load fails, so they see "Couldn't load this photo". No special screen.
