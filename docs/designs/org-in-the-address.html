1. The address in the browser

Every app page starts with /o/{slug}/. Each tab now holds its own organisation. The sidebar, the bell and every count follow the tab, not the browser. Nothing in the app's own screens changes on desktop.

What to notice

  1. Two tabs, two organisations: "Tasks · Acme Studio" and "Tasks · Northwind Bakery". The tab titles already end in the org name today, so they tell the tabs apart without a change.
  2. Each sidebar shows its own org at the top, and its own Notifications count: 3 in Acme, 1 in Northwind. Chat counts are separate too.
  3. Raj adds a task in each window. Neither is sent to Home. No notice appears.
  4. The address bar is the only new thing on screen: /o/acme-studio/tasks.

Phones

  1. Safari on iPhone shows only collab.rajgurung.me until you tap the bar. So the address can't tell Shusma where she is. The page has to.
  2. New: for people in two or more organisations, every phone page names its org in a small line above the title: "Acme Studio", then "Tasks". Home already shows the name, so Home stays as it is.

2. The switcher: rows become links

Each row is now a plain link to the same section in the other organisation. Tasks stays Tasks. From an item page, it goes to that section's list. Because rows are links, Cmd-click, middle-click and long-press open the other org in a new tab, with no extra work.

Decisions

  1. No visual change to the rows. Same initial, name, role and tick as today. The hover fill is the same.
  2. The destination is not printed in the menu. The browser already shows it at the bottom left on hover, as for any link.
  3. The row you are in stays a button with a tick. Clicking it closes the menu, as today. Waiting rows stay disabled. "New organisation" is unchanged.
  4. A plain click opens it in this tab. The row shows a spinner and "Opening…" while the page loads. Cmd, Ctrl, Shift or middle-click leave the menu alone, so no spinner is left behind.
  5. The toast "You're now in Northwind Bakery" stays for a plain click. A new tab gets no toast: the sidebar already says where you are.
Question for Raj: an "open in new tab" hint?None (recommended). People who Cmd-click already know they can. An arrow icon on each row (drawn below, struck out) adds noise to a menu opened every day, and looks like the row leaves the app. If people ask, a tooltip can come later.

4. Not found

Two different pages. The plain one is for an organisation you can't open. The in-org one is for an item missing inside an org you are in.

Desktop · Kreepa opens Northwind (not hers) and a made-up org · the same page
Phone · /o/northwind-bakery/tasks or /o/made-up-name/tasks

Not your org, or no such org

  1. One page for both. Same words, same status (404), same layout, same timing. So the address can't be used to test which organisations exist.
  2. Auth layout, not the app layout. Showing Acme's sidebar under a Northwind address would say she is somewhere she isn't.
  3. Copy: "We couldn't find that page". Then "The link may be wrong, or it may be for an organisation you're not in. Ask whoever sent it to check." It is true for both cases, so it gives nothing away.
  4. "Signed in as kreepa@acmestudio.co.uk" with "Use another account". The commonest real cause is the wrong account on a shared laptop. It reveals nothing about the org.
  5. "Go to Home" opens Home in her last org.
  6. Signed out, every /o/{anything} goes to sign-in first, real or made up. This page only ever shows after sign-in.
Question for Raj: show the signed-in email?Yes (recommended). It is her own email, and "Use another account" fixes the most likely mistake in one tap. Leave it off if you would rather this page say as little as possible.

5. The Home notice after a refused save

"You switched organisation in another tab" can't happen any more: each page posts to its own org's address. The check stays as a backstop (brief question 3). It fires in one real case: a tab opened before this deploy saves to an old bare address. Bare POSTs are refused, never redirected, so nothing lands in the wrong org.

When it still happens
1 · Before the deploy/tasksRaj has Northwind Bakery's Tasks open in a tab, then leaves it.

6. Connect Claude to a chosen organisation

The Allow page asks which organisation when you have two or more. It starts on the one you used last. /mcp and the connector URL don't change. Connect Claude moves to /o/{slug}/settings/claude and lists only that org's connections.

Desktop · 1440 · Raj, two orgs, Acme Studio picked by default
Phone · Northwind Bakery picked
Phone · many orgs

Decisions

  1. Real radio buttons, drawn as 60px cards like the app's status options: initial, name, and "Owner · as raj". The username matters: Claude acts as you, and your username can differ per org.
  2. Starts on the last-used org, marked "Last used". One tap to change.
  3. Only active memberships. Waiting, removed and declined orgs aren't listed.
  4. What Claude can do sits under the picker as two short lines, the same words as today, with "there" in place of the org name. No JavaScript is needed to keep it right.
  5. The buttons stay "Allow" and "Deny".
  6. If the picked org can't be used any more when you press Allow, the page comes back with an error at the top and the list without it. Nothing is connected.
  7. Keyboard: Tab into the group, arrow keys move between orgs (native radios). Focus ring is the app's amber outline.
Question for Raj: name the org on the button?"Allow" only (recommended). "Allow in Northwind Bakery" is clearer, but it needs JavaScript to follow the picker and is long on a phone. The checked card is right above it.

Acting as super admin: the existing oauth/acting.html page is unchanged. The picker only lists orgs you really belong to.

7. A link in an email, on a phone

Shusma's last org was Acme Studio. Raj mentions her on a Northwind Bakery task. She is signed out on her phone. She taps the email, signs in, and lands on the task in Northwind, with Northwind named at the top.

1 · The email (link carries the org)
2 · Sign in (recommended copy)
3 · The task, in Northwind Bakery

Decisions

  1. The email's links point at /o/northwind-bakery/tasks?open=58 and /o/northwind-bakery/notifications. The email's words don't change.
  2. Sign-in says "Sign in, then we'll open the task you tapped." The kind of page (task, chat, notifications, login activity) comes from the address's shape alone. The org is never looked up or named on a public page.
  3. After sign-in she goes straight to the task. No Home in between, no chooser.
  4. On arrival, two things name Northwind: the line above "Tasks" (section 1), and the top of the task sheet, "Northwind Bakery" above the project, shown for people in two or more orgs.
  5. No "You're now in" toast. She didn't switch; she opened a link.
  6. Signed in already: step 2 is skipped. Signed in as an account that isn't in Northwind: the plain not-found page from section 4.
Question for Raj: "Sign in to open a task in Northwind Bakery"?Don't name the org (recommended). Naming it means looking up the slug on a public page, so a real org and a made-up one would get different pages. That is the test section 4 closes. It also keeps next a pure text check, which the security review will prefer. Shusma knows which email she tapped. The org is named the moment she is in.
Question for Raj: name the org in the task sheet?Yes, for people in two or more orgs (recommended). On a phone the sheet covers the header, so without it the org isn't on screen when the task opens.
