1. A conversation

#launch, 8 members, Wednesday morning. Each message is now one block: the bubble holds the text, the time and ticks, and the thread footer. Reactions sit on the bubble's bottom edge, one chip per emoji with its count.

Phone · 390

Decisions

  1. Time and ticks move inside the bubble, bottom right, like WhatsApp. The last line keeps a small gap so text never runs under them.
  2. Every message shows its time. It costs no extra row now, so a run no longer needs to hide it.
  3. Ticks: grey when sent, amber-brown (--link) when everyone has read it, a clock while sending. Amber itself is too faint on the own bubble.
  4. Each emoji gets its own small chip with its own count ("👍 4 · 🎉 3 · ❤️ 1"), most used first, so you can see how many people picked each one. The chips sit in one row on the bubble’s bottom edge and wrap if there are many.
  5. Chips start 10px in from the bubble's left edge on every message, away from the time. On your messages the faces of who saw it sit at the right end of the same row (section 8). The bubble gets 5px more at the bottom so the chips never cover the time.
  6. The chip you reacted with is amber. The others stay neutral.
  7. Each chip is 26px to look at but has a 44px tap area. Tapping a chip shows who reacted with it. The avatar lifts to stay level with the bubble.
  8. Thread replies become a footer inside the bubble under a hairline: faces, "3 replies", time of the last reply. 44px tall, a real link.
  9. In a run, the joined corners tighten (6px) so the run reads as one block.
  10. The smiley sits inside the message field on the left, like WhatsApp.

5. Emoji picker

Opens from the smiley in the chat composer, the thread composer and the task Notes box. A pick goes in at the cursor and the picker stays open for more.

Phone · open, after two picks
Phone · searching "party"
Phone · no match
Phone · first time, nothing recent

Decisions

  1. Phone: the picker docks where the keyboard was, under the composer, so you see each emoji land. The smiley turns into a keyboard button to switch back. No scrim; the chat stays visible.
  2. Desktop: a popover above the smiley, 376px wide. It stays open after a pick; Esc, the smiley or a click outside closes it.
  3. Search first, because it is the fastest way to a specific emoji. Typing filters by name ("party", "thumbs").
  4. "Recently used" shows your last 16. It is hidden, with its tab, until you have picked something. Kept on the device, not the server.
  5. Eight category tabs, 44px each. Inactive tabs are greyed so the strip stays calm; the open one is in colour with an amber underline.
  6. Desktop shows the focused emoji's name at the bottom, which helps keyboard use (arrow keys move, Enter inserts).
  7. No loading state: the emoji list ships with the page script, so it opens instantly.

6. Task notes: reactions, @everyone and emoji

Task "Write launch release notes" in Website launch. Notes stay bubble-less (avatar, name, text) and get the same six reactions in the same pill.

Phone · reacting to sam's note
Phone · typing "@"
Phone · emoji picker in Notes
Phone · no notes, post failed

Decisions

  1. Notes stay bubble-less. A bubble per note would make the sheet look like chat and crowd it.
  2. The reaction pill sits right under the note text, aligned with it. Same pill, same colours as chat; tapping it opens a "Reactions" list like the message sheet.
  3. Each note gets a quiet smiley-plus button at the end of its name row, always visible (no hover on phones). It opens the six picks in a small bar above it.
  4. @ menu: "everyone" first with "Notify the whole team (7 people)", a line, then teammates with their avatars. Five teammates show; typing narrows them.
  5. The Notes box gets the same smiley-in-field as chat. On a phone the picker docks under the box and the sheet scrolls so the box stays in view.
  6. A failed post keeps the text in the box and says why under it.

8. Who read it

Changed at Raj's request: the faces of who has read your message stay on each message, as they do today. They sit right under the bubble on its right side. Reaction chips sit on the left side of the same row, pinned to the bubble's edge. Time and ticks stay inside the bubble.

Phone · tapped the faces on your message
Close up

Decisions

  1. Same rule as today: the faces show under the last of your messages in a run, so a run of five messages does not repeat them five times.
  2. Up to three faces, then "+4". Nothing shows until someone has read it.
  3. Phone: tap the faces (44px tap area) to list everyone who read it. Desktop: hover or focus shows the same list. This is today's list, restyled.
  4. One row under your bubble: reaction chips on the left, who saw it on the right. Others' messages have chips on the left too, so reactions are always in the same place. If a short message has many chips, the row widens past the bubble to the left, and the faces stay on the right.
  5. The tick inside the bubble turns amber-brown once everyone in the room has read it. The faces say who.
  6. Direct messages: no faces, as today. The tick inside the bubble shows "read".
  7. Others' messages never show receipts, as today.
