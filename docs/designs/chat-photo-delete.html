1. "Delete photo" in the message sheet

Juniper Tea Co. In #finance (ana, maya, tom), tom means to send a fuel receipt and sends a photo of his passport. He taps the bubble. The sheet he already knows opens, with one new row at the bottom. Frames labelled "dark" stay dark; the rest follow the theme switch at the top.

Phone · tom, his own photo
Phone · maya (admin), tom's photo in #general
Phone · leo (member), tom's photo: no row
Phone · tom, dark

Decisions

  1. One new row, "Delete photo", in its own list block at the very bottom, under Reactions. Apart from Reply in thread, so a thumb heading for Reply can't hit it.
  2. Danger style the app already uses for Documents' Delete row: trash icon and red text, no fill. The solid red button is saved for the confirm.
  3. The row shows only when you may delete: you sent it, or you are an owner or admin in this conversation. Everyone else sees today's sheet, unchanged. No greyed-out row and no "ask an admin" line.
  4. No chevron: it opens a confirm, not a page.
  5. Once the photo is removed the row is gone. The quote then reads "Photo removed · Van fuel £62".
  6. Desktop: the same sheet as a centred dialog, as today.

2. Delete in the full-screen viewer

A direct message between leo and ana. Leo sends a photo with no caption, opens it full screen and sees it's the wrong one. Delete sits next to Download.

Phone · leo, his photo
Phone · ana (owner) on leo's DM photo
Phone · leo (member) on tom's photo: as today
Phone · ana, it was removed while open

Decisions

  1. Phone: a trash icon button right of Download, same 44px size. Desktop: a labelled "Delete" button right of "Download", same outline shape.
  2. Both in coral (#ff8466, dark mode's danger), since the viewer is always dark. Download stays white, so the two never read as a pair of equals.
  3. It shows to the same people as the sheet row. ana is an owner and in the DM, so she sees it too.
  4. Delete is not on the loading or "Couldn't load" screens. You should see what you are deleting.
  5. If someone deletes the photo while you have it open, the viewer stays open and swaps the photo for "Photo removed" and a Close button. Download and Delete go. Closing it on its own would feel like a glitch.
  6. The caption stays in that state, matching the bubble.

3. The confirm

Both doors open the same confirm. It names where the photo goes from and says it can't be undone. In #general it also says the Documents file goes.

Phone · #finance, from the sheet
Phone · #general (maya, admin), dark
Phone · DM, from the viewer
Phone · deleting
Phone · couldn't delete

Decisions

  1. Same build as the other app deletes (Remove member, Documents Delete): bold question, plain line under it, a full-width solid red button, then a secondary button.
  2. The quote at the top (the existing sheet quote) shows whose photo and when, so an admin knows which one.
  3. DM wording: "It's removed for you and ana." A DM has no #name to put in the sentence.
  4. #general adds one sentence: "It's also deleted from Documents." This is the file that may also sit in a project's folder.
  5. From the viewer, the confirm opens over the viewer as a normal sheet in the app's theme. Cancel goes back to the photo.
  6. Deleting: the red button shows a spinner and "Deleting...". Both buttons lock. The sheet can't be dismissed until it's done.
  7. Failure: a red alert in the confirm, the photo stays, Delete is the retry. No toast, because a toast fades.

4. After: "Photo removed"

The message stays with its caption, reactions and thread. The photo area turns into the "Photo removed" line #general already shows. Every open page following the conversation updates live.

Phone · tom, just deleted
Phone · maya's copy, updated live
Phone · leo's DM, photo with no caption
Phone · chat list

Decisions

  1. Bubble: today's .photo-removed line (image icon, italic, muted), then the caption, then the time. No new style.
  2. No "removed by maya". The brief leaves out who removed it.
  3. A photo with no caption leaves a small bubble with "Photo removed" and the time. It stays so the thread and reactions keep their home.
  4. The person who deleted gets a short "Photo deleted" toast. The bubble is far up the feed when you delete from the viewer, so the toast is the only sign it worked. Others get no toast.
  5. Chat list and Home: "tom: Photo removed · Van fuel £62", "You: Photo removed", only when it's the last message.
